# Sermon Topics Taxonomy

Curated topic list with descriptions, fed to the metadata-extraction prompt.
Carried over from the original prototype.

## Assurance

Applied to scriptural teachings on one's assurance of a saving relationship and right standing with God. (how we are assured, things that obstruct assurance, etc)

## Atonement

God's acting in human history to re-establish the original relationship between God and man by dealing with sin. Old Testament typology and New Testament applications of the atoning work of Christ.

## Attributes & Character of God

Nature of God. Essence of God. Features of God.

## Authority

Aspects of the authority of God. Aspects of God's delegated authority in the areas of the home, employers, civil government and within the church.

## Baptism & New Birth

Water baptism. Baptism of the Spirit. The new man. Newness of Life. Transformation. Regeneration.

## Brotherhood

Spiritual brotherhood. Local brotherhood. Fraternal brotherhood.

## Christian Character & Disciplines

Scriptural actions and attributes to be cultivated in the power of the Spirit (such as Love, Humility, Purity, Hope, Longsuffering, Gentleness, Meekness, Temperance, Understanding, Patience, Kindness, etc.). Christian disciplines (such as Bible reading, fasting, scriptural meditation, arising early, self-control, perseverance, etc.)

## Christian Living - Practical

Scriptural instructions/aspects of a purposeful Christian lifestyle.

## Christmas and Birth of Christ

Christmas Day services, Messages on Christmas,

## Communion Services

All services on a lovefeast weekend and any teaching related to Feet Washing, Lord's Supper, Holy Kiss, Bread & Wine, Death & Resurrection of Jesus.

## Consecration

Definition of Consecrate: to set apart, dedicate, or devote, to the service and worship of God

## Contentment

Satisfaction and rest in the providence of God; Instructions against greed & covetousness.

## Creation & Design

Intricacies of the created world that testify to our Creator. Messages reflecting on the literal truths of the Divine creation account.

## Discernment

Messages that encourage spiritual wisdom, and exhort us to become people "who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil". Practical warnings about specific spiritual and sensual delusions and dangers.

## Discipleship & Passing on the Faith

Opportunities & responsibilities in teaching and modeling Christ, in the home, the church, in mentoring, in evangelism.

## Divorce & Remarriage

Instruction in the scriptural context and teachings of Christ regarding divorce and remarriage

## Doctrine

Specific, practical teachings found in scripture that call us to specific practices in the Christian life. Any area of Orthodoxy that the historical teachings of the church have believed in (e.g. Sovereignty of God, Original Sin, Regeneration, Redemption, Sanctification, Adoption, Inerrancy & Infallibility of Scripture, Deity of Christ, Monotheism, Virgin Birth, Jesus is the Only Way, The Trinity, Created in the Image of God, etc.)

## Early Church

Related to thoughts about the early church in the ante-nicene fathers time period which would be Pre-325 or before the Council of Nicea.

## End Times & Prophecy

Related to prophetic teachings in the scripture about the end times and about the second coming of Christ, the time of tribulation, and the millennial reign.

## Expository

Any sermon that goes through a chapter or a passage of scripture word by word, phrase by phrase and/or verse by verse, exposing the deep truths of the scriptures.

## Faith

Details in the nature of, role of and effect of faith in the life. Saving faith. Justifying faith. Examples of faith. Etc.

## False Doctrine & Apostasy

Areas of discernment against teachings that contradict orthodox christian doctrine such as Prosperity Gospel, Positive Thinking, Word of Faith, New Age, LDS/Mormons, Jehovah Witness, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism, Zoroastrianism, Universalism, etc.

## Finances & Possessions

Teaching from any scripture that deals with money and things we own. Teachings of Jesus on how we are to be stewards of his resources and to not lay up treasures on earth but rather to lay up treasures in heaven. This topic would include areas of Giving, Saving & Investing, Spending, Debt, Budgeting, Insurance, and Retirement.

## Forgiveness

Teaching on the responsibility to forgive others as Christ has forgiven us. "Forgive us our debts as we forgive our debtors."

## Glory of God

The manifest beauty and greatness of God's holiness. It is his public attribute of holiness. The manifold perfection of God.

## Grace

Any of the multi-dimensional scriptural aspects of grace. The glorious flow of the benevolent mercy and favor of God that leads us to repentance, and teaches us to reject ungodliness.

## Head Covering

Teaching on the New Testament instruction to men and women regarding the veiling of their heads while praying or prophesying. (includes spiritual protection, honoring spiritual head, etc.)

## Heaven & Hell

Teaching on the eternal aspects of being forever in the presence of Jesus around his glorious throne vs. eternal damnation in the lake of fire with Satan and his demons.

## Historical Teaching

Any aspect of history - Old Testament historical studies, early church history, later and recent church history topics.

## Home & Family

The father's duty to lead and instruct his family in the ways truth by example and by teaching. The women's duty to be a helper to her husband and to nurture and care for the young. Related to raising up children in the fear & honor of the Lord. Following the teaching to "Be fruitful and multiply and replenish the earth."

## Hope & Encouragement

Related to the hope that we have as believers and any general encouragement.

## Israel

The history of the nation of Israel and its place in the future.

## Jesus Christ

His Person, Son of Man, Son of God, His Life on Earth, His Birth, His Death

## Joy

Definition of Joy: An emotion produced the by the Holy Spirit as he causes us to see the beauty of Christ in the Word of God.

## Justification

Teaching from any of the scripture that involves the action of God and His instruction to man in the features that render a person justified before Him. This is not speaking of faith in a general sense but specifically justification in its aspects.

## Kingdom of God

Jesus said, "My Kingdom is not of this world". Any teaching that explains the Kingdom of God vs. the Kingdom of this World.

## Love

God's love for man. Our love for God. Our love for others. Definition for love: Biblical love is moral character: how we act and treat each other. As children of God, we know that this moral character comes from Jesus.

## Marriage

Defense and instruction on God's original design and purpose of Marriage between one man and one woman for life. Instruction in marital roles, commitment and sanctity of marriage. Topics exploring scriptural parallels between natural marriage and the relationship of Christ and his bride.

## Men's Roles

Any teaching that encourages, warns and instructs fathers in their blessed, God-given privilege and responsibilities as a parent, and men in general about their responsibilities of Leadership, Ministry, Work. Men's roles in marriage

## Mercy

God's mercy toward us. Our mercy toward each other, the lost and our fellow man.

## Ministry & God's Calling

Teaching related to the call of God on the life or to a ministry work. (ie. Public ordained ministry, missionary service, local and personal ministry, scriptural examples of the call of God, etc.)

## Modesty

Teaching on the scriptural simplicity in lifestyle. Teaching against broided hair, pearls, gold and costly clothing. Modesty in outward adornment and in spirit. The relationship of attire and outward adornment to the leanings of the heart.

## Music & Singing

Teaching on the role of godly, worshipful, sacred music and singing. Teachings and warnings against unwholesome music.

## Nonconformity to the World

Teaching that we should not be conformed to the world but rather be transformed to Christ by the renewing of our mind. Scriptural instruction against participation in the world's system of politics.

## Nonresistance

We are to love our enemies and do good to them that hate us and pray for them which despitefully use us and persecute us. We are to have suffering love for our enemies. We are not to resist evil but rather respond by doing good.

## Nonswearing

Scriptural teaching to be men and women of impeccable truth with no added words. Swearing not at all. Not in courts of law nor in our daily business dealings. Instruction to the believer in constantly affirm the truth based on the Word of God.

## Obedience

Any teaching that stresses the following of God's commandments, the teachings of Christ and the holy scriptures literally and fully.

## Occult & Witchcraft

Areas of discernment against any participation in the wickedness of the Occult & Witchcraft. Any teaching that talks about the demonic powers and the cosmic powers of this present darkness and the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places. This includes Magic, Wicca, Spiritualism, Psychics, Satanism, Astrology, Secret Societies, Sorcery, Wizards, Mediums, Fortune-tellers, Divination, Necromancy, Soothsaying, Enchantments, etc.

## Outreach, Evangelism, & Missions

Related to Christ's command to go and teach all nations, baptizing them, and teaching them to observe all things that Christ commands starting first in Jerusalem (at home), and in all Judea (our neighboring cities), and in Samaria (our neighboring territories), and unto the uttermost part of the earth (all nations of the world).

## Peace

Topics that touch on the "peace which passeth understanding", the divine calm of the soul and quietness that comes from forgiveness of sin, sonship and connection with God, and the active work of the Holy Spirit in our lives.

## Persecution & Suffering

Concerning the persecution of believers throughout history, around the world today, and persecution in the future. Related to the life of suffering that Jesus calls his followers to.

## Personal Holiness

Instruction to the believer to be holy as God is holy and other dimensions of holiness as it relates to man throughout the scriptures. The scriptural pattern and instructions in this.

## Pilgrims & Strangers

Concerning the idea that we are not citizens of an earthly kingdom but rather that we are just pilgrims and strangers in this world because our citizenship is of another kingdom. We seek a better and heavenly country.

## Prayer

Instruction on the life of prayer. Aspects of personal/private and public/corporate prayer.

## Pride

Instruction on the destructive nature of pride. Definition of Pride: Arrogance, cynical insensitivity to the needs of others, and presumption. Pride is both a disposition/attitude and a type of conduct.

## Promises of God

Teaching that highlights any of the many promises God has made to mankind in general or to specific men. These words of God are often declarative if/then clauses. They are statements of surety that are pillars for faith.

## Repentance & Confession

Teaching on the nature and requirements of repentance. Differences between sorrow for sin after a godly sort and the self-focused sorrow of being found wrong. Teaching that focuses on the 180º nature of turning from sin and fully yielding up self for crucifixion to be raised by Christ in newness of life. Also any aspect of confession with the mouth and its inescapable role in repentance. (i.e. being cleansed, healed, receiving Christ, etc.)

## Resurrection

Teaching related to the resurrection of Jesus Christ and the resurrection power that believers have who believe in Him. Easter services.

## Revival

Teaching related to personal revival or corporate revivals of the church and awakening to the world both from a historical point of view. Also a call to revival and awakening today. Definition of Revival: To cause to live, to bring back to life from a state of slumber and apathy, to stir up or rekindle a fire.

## Sin

Specific teaching on the nature, role and effects of sin in all of its forms.

## Spiritual Gifts

Instruction on the spiritual gifts of individual believers used privately and in the body of the church (such as Exhortation, Giving, Leadership, Service, Apostles, Prophets, Evangelists, Pastors, Teachers, etc.).

## Spiritual Warfare

Instruction on the battle against Satan and sin, the armor and weapons of our warfare against the principalities and powers of darkness.

## Stewardship

Teachings of Jesus on how we are to steward the gifts and resources that he places into our care. Related to our Time, Money, Possessions, Talents, Careers, etc.

## Submission

Texts and themes that highlight the scriptural instruction of submitting to God, to one another and to the headship order established by God and encourage us in that.

## Temptation

Teaching that highlights the devices of Satan in drawing and deceiving man as well as teaching against specific temptations and lusts that we face.

## Thanksgiving

All services on a thanksgiving weekend or any related teaching to the life of thanksgiving or thankfulness that should characterize followers of Jesus. Messages with theme of Thanksgiving

## The Church

Any teachings on the Church or the Ekklesia. The called out assembly. The bride of Christ. The local brotherhood of believers.

## The Cross

Jesus' death on the cross. The roman cross that Jesus was crucified on. The Cross that Jesus calls his followers to carry.

## The Gospel

The good news. The death and resurrection of Jesus. The new constitution or covenant of Jesus. Liberation from Satan, forgiveness of sins, power of the spirit, and eternal life. That Jesus is King of Kings and Lord of Lords.

## The Trinity

Teaching and preaching on the trinitarian aspects of God. Three in one. Jehovah God (The Father), Jesus Christ (The Son) and the Holy Spirit.

## Trust & Trustworthiness

Similar to faith, but different in the aspect of it being a character trait we can nourish toward God and between believers based on the Word of God and the implementation of Christian character in our lives. An inability to trust can also be related to and inability to forgive and/or love.

## Unity

Any of the scriptural themes that speak to the oneness of the body of Christ, the call for purposeful oneness on a local church level, brother to brother, family and home life. The antithesis of division, conflict and brotherly hatred.

## Victory

Any teaching that highlights the victory of Christ past, present and future. Any of the many dimensions of victory in the lives of the disciples and saints as promised throughout scripture.

## Warnings

A teaching that highlights scriptural warnings throughout scripture. Prophetic messages that warn specifically and practically against dangers and evils of the present life and snares in our Christian warfare.

## Women's roles

Any teaching that encourages, warns and instructs mothers in their blessed, God-given privilege and responsibilities as a parent, and women in general about their duties as daughters and singles in a congregation. Women's roles in marriage.

## Word of God

Related to teachings on the Bible as the divinely inspired Word of God. Teachings that the bible is infallible and inerrant. This would include teachings on the sufficiency of scripture. Sola scriptura vs. Soli scriptura vs. Solo scriptura.

## Worldliness

Teachings that highlight the effect of the world upon the church. Specific teachings that practically expose aspects of friendship with the world as enmity with God.

## Worship

Definition of Worship: To revere or adore with dignity, honor, and respect. To bow down in the presence of God. Definition of Exaltation: Refers to the lofty position of God as above all. He is lifted up as King of creation.

## Youth

Topics that specifically address the stage of life after childhood and prior to adulthood. Topics addressed to young folks, the stage of life where energy and passion is high, and when some of life's biggest and most life-altering decisions are made. Teaching that highlights this most critical and opportune stage for setting the direction for a blessed or disastrous future. Instruction to parents, adults and leaders on the importance of youth direction and how it is both guided by and dramatically influential in our homes and churches. Encouragement, nurturing, vision, warnings, etc.
